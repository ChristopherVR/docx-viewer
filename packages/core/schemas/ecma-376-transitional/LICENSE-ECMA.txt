2. Availability of ECMA-376 (as recorded by Apache POI)
-------------------------------------------------------

Source: apache/poi, legal/LICENSE
https://raw.githubusercontent.com/apache/poi/93b6a820f4aaefd7b062615f843799947872cb24/legal/LICENSE

Office Open XML schemas (poi-ooxml-full-*.jar)

    The Office Open XML schema definitions used by Apache POI are
    a part of the Office Open XML ECMA Specification (ECMA-376, [1]).
    As defined in section 9.4 of the ECMA bylaws [2], this specification
    is available to all interested parties without restriction:

        9.4 All documents when approved shall be made available to
            all interested parties without restriction.

    Furthermore, both Microsoft and Adobe have granted patent licenses
    to this work [3,4,5].
